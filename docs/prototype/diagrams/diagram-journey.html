A customer order, from request to report

Each step has an owner, and the platform records what happened
1
Customer asks
Customer

Sends an order request on the website, WhatsApp or by phone.

Order logged with a reference and channel
2
Sales confirms
Sales officer

Checks stock, agrees price, quantity and delivery with the customer.

Status: Confirmed · customer notified
3
Stock reserved
Storekeeper

Reserves or issues the items from the store.

Stock balance updated · low-stock alert if needed
4
Prepared
Farm team

Harvests or picks, then checks quality against the checklist.

Harvest and loss records linked to the order
5
Delivered
Delivery / sales

Packs, labels and dispatches, or hands over for collection.

Status: Dispatched, then Delivered
6
Paid and receipted
Finance officer

Records payment method and reference, then issues the receipt.

Income tagged: Commercial farm
7
Learn and improve
Management

Reviews sales, stock, delivery times and customer feedback.

Dashboard and exports
The customer sees progress updates at steps 2, 5 and 6. Online payment checkout is a later upgrade: the first release confirms stock and delivery before any payment.
