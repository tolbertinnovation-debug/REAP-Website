VAT-ECDC

Victoria A. Tolbert’s
Early Childhood Development Center

Montserrado, Bentol City

The ECDC provides affordable education, early childhood care, and a feeding program to at-risk youth and impoverished children residing in Bentol City and surrounding areas who suffered from the loss of parenting during the Liberian civil wars, Ebola epidemic, and other unfortunate circumstances resulting in orphanhood.
