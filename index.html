A non-governmental, non-profit organization located in Children Home, Bentol City, Montserrado County, Liberia, West Africa, with a mission to provide critical needed tools, educational resources, psycho-social support and practical vocational training needed to restore hope and empowerment for children, orphans, and young adults impacted by the civil wars in Liberia. We empower and equip them to become middle-class technicians, entrepreneurial, self-sustaining and productive members of their community and the society. Since its establishment in 2003, REAP has provided livelihood and trainings for many citizens of Liberia that are currently impacting society. REAP currently has (18) full-time staff and (14) part-time staff in its administrative and academic cycle.
